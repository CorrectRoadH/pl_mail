**Mail in Spam no longer puts cards on the radar.** The **Happening Soon** panel and the strip above
the mail list showed parcels, bookings, codes and GitHub activity read out of mail in Spam, beside
the real ones, though a fake shipping notice is exactly what spam imitates. Cards from a conversation
in Spam are now left out, and moving it back to the inbox brings them back. Mail in the bin keeps
its cards, because deleting a shipping confirmation does not stop the parcel. No schema change.
